# Product Vision

## Vision

InventoryOS will be a flexible inventory and operations management platform designed to track physical inventory across different types of businesses and locations.

The system should answer three fundamental questions:

1. What inventory do we have?
2. Where is it?
3. What happened to it?

## Design Principles

### Industry Agnostic

InventoryOS should not assume that inventory exists only inside warehouses.

A location could represent:

- Warehouse
- Store
- Stockroom
- Office
- Freezer
- Vehicle
- Workshop
- Other physical storage location

### Traceability

Inventory quantities should not change without an explanation.

Receiving, removal, transfers, and adjustments should generate historical records.

### Data Integrity

Inventory operations must protect against invalid or inconsistent inventory states.

### Modular Growth

The initial inventory system should eventually be capable of supporting additional modules without requiring the entire application to be redesigned.

Possible future modules include:

- Warehouse Management
- Purchasing
- Order Fulfillment
- Analytics
- Equipment / Asset Management
- Integrations

## Long-Term Direction

InventoryOS may progress through several stages:

1. Inventory tracker
2. Inventory management system
3. Advanced inventory platform
4. Warehouse / operations management platform
5. Potential multi-tenant SaaS product

These stages are possibilities rather than current requirements.

The immediate priority remains learning software development and completing a reliable core inventory system.
